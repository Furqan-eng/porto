05 About

I build systems
beyond the
interface. Code is only half the job.

I’m Furqan, an active Informatics student at Almuslim University, Faculty of Computer Science. I care about work that touches real infrastructure — billing, attendance, authentication, operations — not just pages that look finished in a mockup.

Most of my time sits between three layers: the system (how data, auth, and modules connect), the interface (how people actually operate that system), and the network underneath (RADIUS, NAS, monitoring) when the product depends on it.

Coursework gives me the foundation. Projects like DigiRadius and QRify force the harder questions: what breaks at scale, what operators ignore, what a screen has to answer in five seconds. I treat those questions as design work, not afterthoughts.
